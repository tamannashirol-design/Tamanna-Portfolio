About

An Indian-Kenyan designer who works across screens and physical form because good design isn’t limited to either.

I’m currently studying at New York University, where I work across UX/product design with emerging technologies. I design to answer the question: who is this actually for, and what do they actually need?

I’m particularly drawn to designing for people who are underserved by existing systems. The projects I have tackled aren’t just design problems. They’re human ones.

My undergraduate background in Industrial Design at San Francisco State University shaped the way I approach UX by teaching me to think beyond interfaces and consider the entire user experience from context and behavior to form and function.
