01 — Context

A cover brief tied
to Earth Day.

Visual Media Alliance is a non-profit supporting printers, design agencies, and small business owners across the industry. Their quarterly publication, Connected, goes out to their entire member network, they wanted a cover that aligned with Earth Day's theme: Sustainable Print & the Planet.

The brief was to design a cover that promoted awareness of the circular economy — specifically how the print industry sits within it. Educational enough to mean something. Striking enough to be picked up.

Sustainable Print & the Planet: Embracing the Circular Economy.

— VMA Connected, Spring 2025 brief
02 — Color Palette

Five colors.
One unmistakable world.

The palette was built around tension: the warmth of orange against the urgency of green. Neither color could exist without the other, orange without green reads as energy; green without orange reads as nature. Together they read as action.

Silver was added as a neutral to represent the industrial and mechanical side of print production.
